WSI Audio Player — Vector Waveform

Drop an audio file on the canvas (or click Load) to render it as a scrolling isometric terrain of spectral lines. Orange-accented lines are detected beat onsets; the visual density follows the frequency response in real time.

Themes: Auto follows local sunset/sunrise (day → Industrial Orange, night → Monochromatic). Pick an explicit theme to override. Monochromatic swaps the orange accent for white and cycles the waveforms through all hues (rate follows detected BPM).

Perspectives: 90° (default iso), 180°, Mirror, Mesh, Ripple — toggle from the Theme (♦) menu. Each reshapes how the ring buffer is projected to the canvas.

Low-end devices auto-demote to perf-low (translucency off) or perf-lowest (grid/vignette off) when sustained FPS dips. Force a tier with ?perf=low / ?perf=lowest / ?perf=high in the URL.

PNG / Video export saves what you see. Debug sliders at top-left (shortcut) let you retune isoOffsetX/Y, heightScale, terrainLines, etc. for screenshot sessions.
